Put finished, professionally mastered songs you love in this folder (wav, mp3, flac, m4a).

Every master is matched to the 3 songs here whose sound is closest to it: their tonal balance
and their loudness. A mix of genres is fine - a rap song gets matched to your rap references,
a ballad to your ballads. 5-15 songs works well. Use high-quality files (WAV or 320 kbps), not
YouTube rips.

The files stay on your computer and are never added to git.
